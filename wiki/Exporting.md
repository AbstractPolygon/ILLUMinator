# Exporting

From the **Render** panel:

- **Save PNG** (Photo): the picture as shown, with all edits, crop, rotation and LUT, enlarged by the Photo *Size* (Native saves it as rendered).
- **Full resolution:** turn it on (or press F), choose a *Scale* and *Detail recovery*, then *Render*. The still uses every pixel the sensor recorded, several times the resolution of the live picture. *Save PNG* then saves the full resolution still.
- **Show 3D:** shows the picture in 3D while you edit, in the chosen *3D type*; turn it off for the normal view.
- **Save 3D image:** a stereo pair from two viewpoints across the lens, enlarged by the Photo *Size*: *Anaglyph* (for red / cyan glasses), *Side by side* or *Cross-eyed*. *Baseline* sets how far apart the two eyes are; more gives a stronger 3D effect.
- **Export video:** the animation (Animation panel, *Enabled*) as a video in the chosen *File format* (WebM or MP4), *Compression* (VP9, VP8, AV1, H.264 or H.265) and *Quality*, at the Video *Size* (1080p, 1440p or 4K: the video's height, its short side) and at the animation's *Frame rate*. The picture follows *Resolution*; with *Full resolution* on, every frame is rendered from the whole sensor with its *Scale* and *Detail recovery* first (much slower: each frame takes as long as a full resolution still).
- **Upscaling:** photos, 3D images and video frames are enlarged with the chosen *Method* (Lanczos, sharp, or Smooth) and *Sharpen*. Enlarging adds pixels, not detail. See [[Render]].
- **Export album:** in the library, **•••** next to an album → *Export album…* saves the album's original photos and edits as one `.zip`, to open in another library. See [[Library and Albums]].

In the desktop app files are saved where you choose; in a browser they go to your downloads.
