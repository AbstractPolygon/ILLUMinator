A modern replacement

Lytro is gone. Your camera is not.

Lytro shut down in 2018, and Lytro Desktop was left behind. ILLUMinator picks up where it stopped: a modern app for Windows, macOS, and Linux, plus a self-hosted version that can run in any modern web browser.

ILLUMinator renders on the GPU, so the application responds quickly as you interact with your photos.

It aims to be production-ready software, built to handle real libraries and finished work, not an experiment.

Superior results

Higher resolution, higher quality.

ILLUMinator renders your light fields at a higher resolution and a higher quality than Lytro Desktop, for stills and for video. Its full resolution renderer places every pixel of the sensor where it belongs, then recovers fine detail in extra passes, up to four times the lens grid. A dedicated upscaler then enlarges photos up to four times more and video frames to 4K, keeping edges crisp, with its method and sharpening yours to set.

Save stills as PNG or JPEG, up to 2460 × 1708 (4.2 MP) as rendered or 9840 × 6832 (67 MP) upscaled, and 3D pictures as anaglyph, side by side or cross-eyed pairs. Export video as WebM or MP4, compressed with VP9, VP8, AV1, H.264 or H.265, from 1080p to 4K or sized for social media at 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9.

Animation

Keyframe everything.

Lytro Desktop could only animate the camera: focus, viewpoint and aperture. In ILLUMinator every slider can change over time: exposure, white balance, the tone curve, each local adjustment's position and effect, tilt, bokeh, lens corrections and framing.

Each setting has a ◆ beside it to add keyframes and step between them, and the keyframe editor shows them grouped like the panels, with smooth curves you can shape.

Living filters

Ready-made looks built from masks that follow the focus: color pop, spotlight, film noir and more.

Depth masks

Adjust only what lies between two depths, using the depth the light field measured.

Tilt-shift

Tilt the plane of focus in any direction after the shot, and spread the sharp range.

3D

Stereo pairs from two viewpoints across the lens, as anaglyph, side by side or cross-eyed.

Upscaling to 4K and beyond

Photos enlarged up to 4× and video up to 4K with a sharp Lanczos upscaler, from frames rendered at full resolution when you want the detail.

Social media sizes

Export video ready for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Stories at 9:16, feed posts at 4:5 or 1:1, and YouTube at 16:9, up to 4K.

Export your way

Video as WebM or MP4 with VP9, AV1, H.264 or H.265 at up to 40 Mbit/s; photos as lossless PNG or JPEG at the quality you choose.

Backward compatibility

Every photo and every edit comes with you.

Point ILLUMinator at your Lytro Desktop library and it brings every picture across, with the edits Lytro Desktop saved for each one, into an album named after the library: color, crop, animations as keyframes, effects as local adjustments, pan and zoom, and 3D. The work you did in Lytro Desktop is still there, and importing the library again replaces photos with the newer copies.

Photos also import straight from the camera over USB. Edits are kept beside each photo in a plain file, can be reset to a fresh start from the photo's menu, and albums export as a single archive to move between computers.
